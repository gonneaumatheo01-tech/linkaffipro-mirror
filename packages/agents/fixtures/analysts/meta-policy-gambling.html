Online gambling and gaming

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Ads that promote online gambling and gaming, where anything of monetary value is required to play and anything of monetary value forms part of the prize, are only allowed with our prior written permission.

Authorised advertisers must target people aged 18 or older and only in the countries where they hold a valid licence. Additional country rules apply: in Germany ads must include a responsible gaming notice; in Spain only brand ads without incentives are allowed; in France ads must display the official warning message.

Ads must not use words such as "free", "guaranteed" or "risk-free" to describe bonuses, and must not imply that gambling can solve financial problems.

Repeated violations may lead to the restriction of the ad account and the Business Manager.
